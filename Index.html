Your support. Someone's second chance.
“Do not measure love by size. Measure it by sincerity.”

inspired with Mr. S.Arun kingsley, 24 years old was normal and healthy boy up - to Feburary 2018. He is a graduate in engineering. He was seeking a job and aiming to become an employer and to support the family to repay the loan of 4 lakh taken for his studies. But heart was broken into fragments when we came to know from the doctor that he was being affected from Acute Myeloid Leukaemia a type of blood cancer and we became again speechless when the doctor told about the cost of treatment around Rs. 25 lakhs.

We had already spent 10 lakh but the doctor gave another estimation for bone marrow transplantation for Rs.15 lakhs. We did not know what to do. We have to save the boy and got cured from this disease. His father was a retired teacher with meagre income. He was unable to meet these expenses. He was in a position to look-after his wife who suffers from Bone cancer spending lot of money for her medication.

That's why we decided to support the case of cancer guidance and financial referral.
