    These are two miserable brother and sister living together with great tribulations. Both of them not married, not working, suffers from incurable diseases. They dwell in a small hut which may fall at any time. Rats and reptiles are also living with burrows in the same house. Very very pathetic situation they need all type of supports like food, cloth, shelter, medical and moral. They depends upon Government aid for their food. But other needs are not met due to diabetics. Our trust is supporting them to purchase medicines and some food materials.
